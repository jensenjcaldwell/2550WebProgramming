Home

Science is the study of how the world works, from the smallest particles to entire galaxies. It helps us ask better questions, test ideas with evidence, and build explanations we can trust.

In this class, we will explore major scientific concepts through observation, experimentation, and discussion. The goal is not just to memorize facts, but to think critically and understand why natural laws matter in everyday life.

Observation

Science begins by noticing patterns, changes, and questions in the world around us.

Experiment

We test ideas with careful procedures, data, and evidence we can measure.

Discovery

Each lesson builds toward a deeper understanding of how nature behaves.

Why study science?

Science teaches us how to solve problems, evaluate evidence, and make informed decisions in a changing world.
